## 1. Hero — Bahrain: Iran strikes US bases

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they carried out a combined
missile-and-drone attack on US military positions at the al-Riffa
and Awali bases in Bahrain, claiming to have hit two Patriot air
defence radars. Bahrain said the attack struck a civilian industrial
facility and started a fire, with no injuries reported, and that its
air defences intercepted other attacks. The Guards said the strike
was retaliation for US drones violating Iranian airspace.

## 2. Mexico — "El Chapo Isidro" captured

Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as
"El Chapo Isidro", one of the FBI's Ten Most Wanted fugitives, in a
raid on a house in Guasave, Sinaloa, on Friday night, with US
intelligence help. The arrest set off gunfights, burning roadblocks
and fires; at least 11 people died — seven alleged cartel gunmen and
four Mexican soldiers. Eight people were detained.

## 3. Ukraine — Missile hits Zelenskyy's hometown

A Russian ballistic strike hit the centre of Kryvyi Rih, President
Zelenskyy's hometown, on Friday night, killing a 7-year-old boy and
a 20-year-old woman and wounding 60 people, including six children.
A second wave of missiles struck while rescuers were at work — a
double-tap attack. About 25 apartment buildings, a hospital and
schools were damaged. Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone had
also landed in Poland the day before.

## 4. Antalya — COP31 climate talks run into overtime

The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, ran past its deadline on
Saturday with no deal. Nearly 200 countries were split over fossil
fuels, climate finance and warming targets, and an earlier draft
deal did not mention fossil fuels at all. France, Panama and other
countries said any deal must include a clear transition away from
them; some decisions may be put off until next year.

## 5. New York — Wall Street slides into correction

US stocks fell sharply on Friday as the sell-off in AI and chip

## 6. Brazil — Mosquitoes that beat dengue

A four-year trial in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, found about 69% fewer
dengue and chikungunya infections among more than 5,700 children in
neighbourhoods where mosquitoes carrying Wolbachia bacteria were
released, compared with similar neighbourhoods. The protection held
even during Brazil's severe 2024 dengue outbreak. Results were
presented on Saturday at the American Society of Tropical Medicine
and Hygiene meeting.

- Associated Press (21 Nov 2026, via LA Post):

- Times of India (21 Nov 2026, AP report):
